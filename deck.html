The 5 Program Initiatives

Detailed breakdown of rationales, execution timelines, budgets, and civic outputs across cinema, performing arts, and heritage staging.

Project 01 • November 2027
International Festival & Film Tourism Budget: ₱25M – ₱35M

Manila City International Film Festival (MCIFF) 2027

The revival, rebranding, and evolution of Manila City’s pioneering 10-day Filipino film festival established by Mayor Antonio J. Villegas in 1966[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span). Globally positioning Manila as a creative hub for Asian and world cinema, cultural diplomacy, and foreign film investment[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

Venues: Metropolitan Theater, Cinematheque Manila, Intramuros, Robinsons Manila, Lucky Chinatown, SM Manila & San Lazaro[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span)
Funding Sources: City Government of Manila, National Film/Cultural/Tourism Agencies, Corporate Sponsors, Benefactors[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)
Sec. General: Francis O. Villacorta Sponsor Festival
Project 02 • Continuing Program
Talent Development & Youth Empowerment Budget: ₱10M – ₱15M Year-Round

Manila Community Acting Workshop

Democratizing access to professional theater and acting education[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span). Conducting decentralized training in voice, movement, and performance across Manila's barangays and districts for students, out-of-school youth, and community artists—culminating in public district showcases and the Manila City Actor's Company[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

Schedule & Cycle: 6 weekend classes for 8 weeks per cycle covering all Manila districts with showcase culminations[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span)
Funding Sources: City of Manila, Sangguniang Kabataan (SK), Cultural Grants, Institutional Partners, Corporate Sponsors[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span)
Director: Francis O. Villacorta Support Workshop
Project 03 • 6–8 Months Prep
Original Heritage Musical Budget: ₱8M – ₱10M

Battle of Bangkusay, A Heritage Musical

A major theatrical production transforming a defining yet insufficiently dramatized turning point in Manila's history[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span). Showcasing the courage of Manila’s early defenders, maritime commerce roots, and cultural resistance through elaborate musical staging and nation-building narrative[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).

Staging Venues: Metropolitan Theater, Liwasang Bonifacio, Luneta[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span)
Funding Sources: City of Manila, Cultural Grants, Institutional Partners, Corporate Sponsors, Cultural Patrons[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span)
Director: Francis O. Villacorta Co-Produce Musical
Project 04 • 3 Cycles / Year
Filmmaker Incubation & Production Budget: ₱10M – ₱15M

Manila City Film Production Lab

Nurturing Manila's student filmmakers and young creatives from concept to completed movies[span_12](start_span)[span_12](end_span). Intensive workshops, project development, scriptwriting, and production funding to capture Manila’s rich history, people, and contemporary realities[span_13](start_span)[span_13](end_span).

Key Deliverables: Completed screenplays, developed projects, short films, proof-of-concept movies, Manila film archive[span_14](start_span)[span_14](end_span)
Funding Sources: City of Manila, Cultural Institutions, Production Companies, Corporate Sponsors, Cultural Benefactors[span_15](start_span)[span_15](end_span)
Director: Francis O. Villacorta Partner With Lab
Project 05 • 8–10 Months Prep
40th Anniversary Commemorative Musical Budget: ₱10M – ₱15M

Lorenzo de Manila, The Musical

Commemorating the 40th anniversary of Saint Lorenzo Ruiz's sainthood[span_16](start_span)[span_16](end_span). Celebrating his faith, family, and martyrdom while honoring the Filipino-Chinese community’s massive contribution to Binondo's trading history and modern nation-building through full-scale theater with LED film material integration[span_17](start_span)[span_17](end_span).

Venues: Metropolitan Theater, Binondo Plaza, Lucky Chinatown Mall[span_18](start_span)[span_18](end_span)
Funding Sources: City of Manila, Filipino-Chinese Chamber of Commerce, Corporate Sponsors, Tourism/Religious Partners[span_19](start_span)[span_19](end_span)
Director: Francis O. Villacorta Sponsor Musical
